# BusinessBits

BusinessBits is an AI business partner for one or two person UK businesses, such as consultancies and agencies. It is a set of skills that do the everyday work of running the business, grouped by business area and the role inside it. Revenue is the first area.

The skills draft and record. You approve and send. Nothing leaves the business without your yes.

## Skills

| Skill | What it helps you do |
| --- | --- |
| [Business setup](setup/business-setup/SKILL.md) | Set up the other skills for your business: where your records live, your offer and prices, who you sell to, how you write, and the basic UK registrations. |
| [Enquiry](revenue/sales/enquiry/SKILL.md) | Decide what to do with an inbound enquiry, record it once and draft a reply for you to send. |
| [Prospecting](revenue/sales/prospecting/SKILL.md) | Find and research companies worth contacting, check every contact, and draft outreach you send yourself. |
| [Proposal](revenue/sales/proposal/SKILL.md) | Price the work, write the proposal and statement of work, and handle changes once a project is signed. |
| [Pipeline](revenue/sales/pipeline/SKILL.md) | Review open deals each week: what to chase, what to close, and whether you have time to deliver. |

Start with Business setup. It writes your business profile, and the other skills read it.

## How it's organised

Folders go area, then role, then skill: `revenue/sales/proposal` is the proposal skill in the sales role of the Revenue area. Business setup sits on its own because every area uses the profile it writes.

The method lives in the skills and is the same for every business. Your own details (prices, buyers, voice, how you chase) live in your business profile, which stays with you and never goes in this repo. Where a skill has a default, such as how many chasers before letting a deal go, the profile can change it.

The skills share one set of [record definitions](RECORDS.md). Each skill carries its own copy, so any one of them works when used alone.

## Using one skill

Each skill folder is complete on its own. Give your assistant the folder, or ask it to read the SKILL.md file in that folder and the files beside it. See the [compatibility notes](../docs/compatibility.md) for what has and hasn't been tested.

Every skill states what it produces and where it goes, the step it takes off your plate, and what it never does without your yes.
