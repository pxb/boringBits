# Statement of work template

Fill every section. Anything not written here is not included.

```text
STATEMENT OF WORK [number]
Client: [client legal name]            Date: [date]
Under the terms of business dated [date]

1. Background
   The problem in the client's words, and why it matters.

2. Objective
   One sentence: what will be true when this is done.

3. Scope
   Included: [the bounded list]
   Not included: [the exclusions, plus anything discussed and ruled out]

4. Deliverables
   [numbered list; each one something the client can see or use]

5. Client responsibilities and dependencies
   [access, data, people, decisions, each with a date]
   Owner: [the client's named person for this work, its result and its
   upkeep, and who approves changes].
   If any of these is late, the timeline moves by the same amount.

6. Assumptions
   [anything the price depends on]

7. Timeline
   Start [date], milestones [dates], acceptance by [date].

8. Acceptance
   Test: [how both sides know it is done]
   The client has [5] working days after delivery to accept or list
   defects against the deliverables. [2] revision rounds are included.

9. Price and payment
   [Fixed price / day rate / retainer]: [amount] [VAT statement].
   Payment: [milestones]. Invoices due in [14] days.
   Third-party costs: [billed to the client's own accounts at cost].
   Not in this price: [the exclusions from section 3, in one line].

10. Changes
    Anything not in sections 3 and 4 is a change. We send a change note
    within two working days with the price and the effect on timeline
    and acceptance. Work starts once the client approves it in writing;
    an email reply counts. Approved notes are added below as amendments.
    If scope shrinks, the price comes down on the same basis.

11. After acceptance
    Defects fixed for [30] days after go-live. A defect is a deliverable
    not doing what this statement of work says. New requests, changed
    rules and changes in the client's own systems are changes, not
    defects. After that: [client-operated / support agreement].

Amendments
    [1] [date] [what changed] [price] [approved by, date]

Signed for the client: ____________   Signed for [business]: ____________
```
