# Meeting prep

A call with no brief is a call where the first ten minutes are spent recovering what was already known.

## 1. Get what you need

- the calendar entry: attendees, time, who called it
- every record about the attendees' organisations: enquiries, previous work, open proposals, unpaid invoices
- what the business has decided not to offer

If a record does not exist, say so in the brief. An empty field is information; a guessed field is a trap.

## 2. Write the brief

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Who is on the call | Each attendee, their role, and why they are in the room |
| How we got here | The enquiry, referral or previous work, with dates |
| What they want | As they stated it, and what that implies they will pay for |
| Open items | Questions asked and unanswered, in both directions |
| What to ask | Three questions whose answers change the proposal |
| What not to promise | Price, date, scope, or a capability not yet proved |
| Risks | What would make this a bad deal |
| Decision | What should be true when the call ends |

One page. If it runs longer, the brief is doing the job of the records.

## 3. Choose the three questions

Pick questions where a different answer leads to a different proposal, not questions that fill in a form. Typical shapes:

- what happens if they do nothing?
- who else has to agree, and what do they care about?
- what has already been tried, and why did it stop?

## 4. Fill "what not to promise" every time

This row is the point of the exercise. List the things this business is tempted to say yes to on a call and should not: a discount, a date that depends on someone else, a capability nobody has delivered, an integration nobody has scoped.

## 5. Check before you finish

- every statement traces to a record, or is marked unknown
- every attendee appears, with a reason they are there
- the brief exists before the call, not after
- no price or date in the brief that has not been approved

## 6. Never

- present a guess about the prospect's situation as fact
- summarise the relationship from memory when the records are available
- leave "what not to promise" empty
- write a brief that could apply to any prospect

## 7. Hand off

- to [enquiry](../enquiry/SKILL.md) when the call reveals a new requirement
- to the quote conversation when scope becomes clear enough to price
