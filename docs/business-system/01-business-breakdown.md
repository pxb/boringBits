# The business, in the founder's own words

Written 2026-11-16. This is the directive. Everything else in this directory is this made specific. If another document contradicts it, that document is wrong.

> Revenue: Marketing + Sales (IB and OB lead, sales, funnel, pipeline all the way to close, then account management and customer success (retention)
>
> Delivery: Software factory, sub agent delegation, PRD, Design Systems, Architecture.md, everything needed to ship efficiently and quickly and product manage. Capture functional requirements, break down into product requirements document, break down project into chunks and actions, orchestrate and delegate, code, review, UAT, ship. Then maintenance, updates, breakages, fixes, etc.
>
> Company: Finance, invoices, billing, legal
>
> Direction: Planning, Decision Making, Market Trends, Analysis (CEO, CFO).
>
> The traditional job titles help us visualise the role, objective and scope.

## How it becomes five tracks

Delivery splits in two. Client delivery ends with access removed, acceptance and a closure record. Product building repeats: a release is followed by another release, and then by maintenance. Different objects, different owners, different clocks.

| Track | Purpose | Owns | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Turn attention into work won or lost deliberately | enquiry record, proposal terms, pipeline view, outbound log, published content, account plans | prices without a pricing model, sends without approval of that message, promises a date it cannot fit |
| Deliver | Turn a yes into work accepted | agreed requirements, engagement state, acceptance record, closure record, support outcomes | marks work accepted without the client's own run, closes with access unsettled |
| Build | Turn requirements into a shipped product, then keep it alive | product requirements, architecture and decisions, design rules, task chunks, verification verdict, release record | ships without evidence at the revision, lets a deviation pass review unrecorded |
| Company | Keep the entity solvent, legal, insured and recoverable | cash position, invoices, liabilities, filings, contracts, obligations, estate, risks | invents a number, treats a missing statement as zero, deletes under a hold |
| Direction | Decide what the business does and stops | decision register, plan and targets, market signals, read of the numbers | decides price, hiring or a legal position without a person |

## Roles place the reader

Each track is divided into roles with an objective and a scope. A founder reads "customer success" and knows immediately whether that is their week. Skills hang off roles, and the audit fails if a role has no skill or a skill sits on a role that does not exist.

The roles are on [02-skill-map.md](02-skill-map.md). They are a way to read the work, not a headcount plan.
