# Product requirements

What is being built, for whom, and how we will know it works. One document per product or per substantial release.

## Problem

The situation today, in the user's words. What it costs them.

## Outcome

What changes when this ships. Observable, not aspirational.

## Who it is for

The user, their context, what they already do instead.

## Requirements

Numbered, each testable. A requirement that cannot fail is not a requirement.

| # | Requirement | Acceptance criterion | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|

## Non-goals

What this explicitly does not do. Written down so it is not relitigated mid-build.

## Constraints

Platform, budget, data, dependency, regulatory, deadline.

## Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|

## Open questions

| Question | Owner | Needed by |
|---|---|---|

## Release

How it reaches users, how it is measured, how it is undone.

## Definition of done

Every requirement either meets its criterion with evidence or is explicitly deferred with a reason.
